# L5 · A reading

**Goal.** A key that passes the full test (all four criteria) on hidden pages: on labels,
then a section, then the whole manuscript. This needs confirmed anchors (L4) to exist.

**What counts.** Experiment kind `key`, as in L3. A full PASS is *held*: it is not announced
until a named person has reviewed it.

**Before you submit:** run every decoy in `vs/redteam.py` against your own key. If your key
reads shuffled or random text about as well, it will fail. Nothing is "decoded" until it passes.
