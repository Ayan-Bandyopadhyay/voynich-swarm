# L2 · Facts

**Goal.** Structural claims about the text, each pre-registered (metric + threshold fixed in
the PR) and scored **once** on the hidden pages. Refuted claims count like established ones.

**What counts.** Experiment kind `fact`, file `fact.json` with a spec in the measurement language
(vs/dsl.py: scope, selection, statistic, comparisons to thresholds, reference languages and nulls):
`{"title", "statement", "source", "spec": {"scope": {"hand": "1"}, "select": {"unit": ["L"]},
 "statistic": {...}, "compare": [{"null": "shuffle_labels", "perms": 400, "p": 0.01}]}}`.
Nulls matter: a claim that survives only a weak comparison is weak. Scope it to a scribe or
dialect where the effect lives (the scribes differ). It must also hold on Takahashi's
transliteration (else FRAGILE). Built-in metrics in vs/facts.py still work but specs are preferred.
Rehearse as much as you like on the public practice split (`python3 -m vs experiment ...`);
the held-out verdict is final and the claim is never edited. A sharper claim is a new PR.

**Tools.** Metrics in `vs/facts.py` (word-length skew, Zipf slope, conditional entropy h2,
slot grammar cover, paragraph/line position effects, Currier A/B and section classifiers,
label onsets, adjacent repeats and near-repeats, word entropy, A/B vocabulary divergence,
glyph-order rigidity, repeated 4-grams, hapax share, adjacent-word mutual information,
same-page reuse, copy-and-tweak). Reference languages: `vs/refcorpus.py` (pinned texts).

**Publicly known.** Low conditional character entropy (Bennett 1976); languages A and B,
line and paragraph effects (Currier 1976); section vocabularies (Montemurro & Zanette 2013);
self-similar words (Timm & Schinner 2020); very few repeated phrases (Claude "Voynich Text
Examination", 2026). Registering a known fact is fine: the point is that it is scored on
hidden pages and that the red team then attacks it.

**Good claims** say something a language would or would not do, with a threshold derived from
the reference corpora, not tuned to the manuscript.
