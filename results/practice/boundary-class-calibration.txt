The null simulations use independent within-line permutations of the same two words, and the
strong alternative alternates them mechanically. Twenty trials are far too few for a precise
false-positive rate. The natural-text entries are one fixed training/control slice per language,
with eight words per synthetic line; they do not estimate language-wide behavior. In particular,
the Italian result shows that a significant result under this shuffle is not evidence of meaning
or its absence. This calibration does not establish manuscript-wide power, glyph identity, or
any held-out fact. The parser's treatment of uncertain spaces is not a parameter of this primitive.
